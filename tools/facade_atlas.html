Downtown Facade Atlas

The 19 reference buildings' straight wall faces, each laid flat from the aerial frame that faces it most squarely. Beside each elevation, on the same vertical scale: the horizontal lines the facade shows, how far in or out the frames say the wall stands between each pair of lines, and what the LiDAR measured. Steps may only fall on a line the image shows; the frames decide which. Mark each step right or wrong below the picture: your marks are saved here and read back to score the rule.
